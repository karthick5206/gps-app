Delete your VeeTrax account

For the VeeTrax app (com.sasvata.veetrax), the VeeTrax Tracker app (com.sasvata.veetrax.tracker) and app.veetrax.com, provided by Vibhav Enterprise Solutions, Chennai, India.

How to delete your account

  1. In the VeeTrax app: open More, tap your name at the top (Profile), scroll to Delete account, type the confirmation and tap Delete my account.
  2. On the web: open app.veetrax.com/delete-account, sign in, type the confirmation and press Delete my account.
  3. VeeTrax Tracker users: the phone belongs to the VeeTrax account of the e-mail or mobile number used to activate it. Sign in on the web page above with that e-mail, or with an OTP sent to that mobile number, and delete the account. To only stop sharing your location, tap Sign out / deactivate this phone in the Tracker app.
  4. Can't sign in? Call +91 94449 07658 or e-mail info@sasvatamobility.com from your registered e-mail address or mobile number, and we will delete the account for you within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Personal or family account: the whole account – your login, every phone, GPS tracker and dashcam in it, and all their locations, route history, trips, alerts, reports and settings.
  • User of a company account: your login and personal settings. The company's vehicles and data belong to the company; ask your administrator to delete them.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Encrypted backups that may still contain your data are removed automatically within 100 days.
  • Invoices for paid licences are kept as long as Indian tax law requires.
