# 07 · VeeTrax Web Design System: Audit and Proposed Token Structure

**Date:** 2026-11-09 · **Codebase:** `main` @ `17a2e63` · **Method:** read-only source review plus computed WCAG 2.x contrast ratios (sRGB relative luminance; alpha tints composited over the actual card colour). Nothing in the app was changed.
**Inputs:** [Phase 1 web screens & design](../discovery/07-web-screens-and-design.md) (§3, F-04, F-07 to F-10) · [Phase 2 visual assessment](../discovery/phase2/VISUAL_ASSESSMENT.md) (X6, colour and accessibility rows).
**Product constraints (fixed):** keep the VeeTrax logo exactly · keep the Teal, Plum and Sky families and the Light and Dark themes · refine only for consistency, contrast and accessibility · use Inter and make sure it actually loads · target WCAG 2.1 AA.

Evidence labels: **Confirmed** = read in source or computed from source values. **Inferred** = reasoned from code without running the app.

### 4.2 Interpretation

1. **StatusBadge (light themes) — highest impact.** [status-badge.tsx:21](../../apps/web/src/components/status-badge.tsx#L21) uses the raw marker colour as text on a 14 % tint of itself. 2. **`Badge` success/warning/destructive** miss 4.5 by a small margin (4.01 to 4.27) because they use `bg-x/12 text-x` rather than the existing soft tokens, which pass at 5.7 to 8.3. **Confirmed.**
3. **Dark destructive button:** white on `#ef4444` is 3.76. **Confirmed.**
4. **Focus ring:** buttons, tabs, checkboxes and switches draw only `ring-[3px] ring-ring/40` (inputs and selects also switch to a solid `border-ring`, which passes). The 40 % ring is 1.6 to 2.6:1 against the page. **Confirmed.** WCAG 2.1 AA 1.4.11 applies to focus indicators. **Inferred** (common interpretation).
5. **Input borders:** at 1.46 to 1.54, the field boundary is barely visible on white cards. Fields also sit on `bg-card` with `shadow-xs`, and the placeholder and label help identify them, so this is a **1.4.11 risk rather than a clear failure**. **Inferred.**
6. **Passes worth keeping:** every theme's body, muted, primary-button, header and tooltip pairs clear AA comfortably. Sky's primary (5.80) and Sky/Light header muted (4.70/4.75) have the least headroom. **Confirmed.**

### Appendix: method notes
- Contrast: WCAG 2.x relative luminance; `(L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05)`. Alpha tints (`/12`, 14 % `color-mix` with `transparent`, Dark `rgb(… / 0.15)` soft tokens) composited in sRGB over the theme's `--card`. Minimal fixes were found by mixing the foreground toward black (light backgrounds) or white (dark backgrounds) in 1 % steps until the threshold was met, then rounded to a nearby palette-consistent value.
- Hex counts: `grep -oE "#[0-9a-fA-F]{6}"` over `apps/web/src/**/*.{ts,tsx,css}` on `17a2e63`.
- Not verified at runtime: rendered fonts per OS (taken from Phase 2 screenshots), MultiSelect chip keyboard handling, and exact visual impact of px text under the Large text setting.
